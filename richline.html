Twenty-Five Years in the Making

From housing finance to World Will Glow

Richline is built on a career spent understanding how Indian businesses buy, sell and trust — long before the first LED shipped.

2001

Foundations in real estate & housing finance

Ravi Adwani began his career in real estate and housing finance — learning how trust, credit and long-term relationships are built with dealers and customers alike.

June 2004

A move into telecom

A shift into the telecom industry brought exposure to distribution networks and channel sales at scale — groundwork for a future manufacturing and dealer business.

2016

SSAKAR founded — Future Green launches

Drawing on 15 years across finance and telecom, Ravi founded SSAKAR and launched Future Green, an LED lighting brand manufactured in Bhopal for dealers and distributors nationwide.

2026

Future Green is now Richline

A decade of promises kept — the same factory, team and catalogue now carried forward under a registered name and identity: Richline. World Will Glow®.

Richline Assurance

What we commit, we deliver.

Manufactured in Bhopal, backed by a decade of dealer relationships. Our assurance isn't a slogan — it's a standard we hold every batch to.

Non-Negotiable 01

Would we run it in our own house?

Every panel, COB or bulb we ship is one we'd fit in our own home or office first. If we wouldn't live with it, we don't put the Richline name on it.

Non-Negotiable 02

Is it worth every rupee spent?

Real value for every rupee the end user pays — no cutting corners to hit a price point, and no premium tag for a logo alone. If it doesn't earn its price, it doesn't ship.
